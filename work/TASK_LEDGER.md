# TASK_LEDGER.md — Optional status register

Use only if a project needs durable tracking across multiple substantial tasks.

| ID | Outcome | Status | Owner | Evidence / Next trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (task ID) | (short goal) | open | (person/role) | (link or next action) |

Allowed statuses: `open`, `blocked`, `deferred`, `closed`. Report a status as closed only when supported by actual evidence.

`TASK_REPORT.md` is required and owned and approved by the manager from executor evidence; an explicitly assigned reporting helper may draft or write it. It records outcomes but does not independently change this ledger's status. Do not treat a chat summary as authoritative status.
